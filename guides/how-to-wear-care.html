Energizing & Maintenance

How to Wear & Care

The complete Vedic and gemological guide to wearing, energizing and maintaining Gemstones & Rudraksha.

How to Wear Gemstones

Gemstones are energetic amplifiers. For astrological benefits, they must be energized and worn correctly based on planet, metal, finger and weight.

Best Day & Time

  • Sunday – Ruby (Sun)
  • Monday – Pearl (Moon)
  • Tuesday – Red Coral (Mars)
  • Wednesday – Emerald (Mercury)
  • Thursday – Yellow Sapphire (Jupiter)
  • Friday – Diamond / Opal (Venus)
  • Saturday – Blue Sapphire / Hessonite / Cat’s Eye (Saturn / Rahu / Ketu)

Which Finger?

  • Ring finger – Yellow Sapphire, Ruby, Diamond
  • Middle finger – Blue Sapphire, Amethyst
  • Little finger – Emerald, Pearl
  • Index finger – Red Coral, Hessonite (conditional)

Step-by-Step Energizing Method

  1. Place the gemstone in a clean bowl with Ganga jal + raw milk + honey + curd + pure water (Panchamrit).
  2. Rinse gently with pure water and wipe with a clean, soft cloth.
  3. Place on a copper or silver plate facing morning sunlight or a lit ghee diya.
  4. Chant 108 times: “Om Hreem Namah” or the specific planetary mantra while focusing your positive intention.

How to Cleanse Gemstones

  • Running natural spring water (avoid for very soft or porous stones).
  • Pure incense smoke, guggal or sandalwood dhoop.
  • Moonlight bath (Full Moon / Purnima is ideal for rejuvenation).
  • Sound cleansing (Tibetan singing bowl, bell harmonics, or Vedic chanting).
  • Dry salt or salt bed cleansing (avoid for Pearl, Coral, Opal & Emerald).

How to Wear Rudraksha

  1. Best day: Monday or Pradosh / Maha Shivratri / any Shiva-related tithi.
  2. Clean gently with pure water, dry thoroughly, then apply a drop of natural sandalwood oil or paste.
  3. Chant 108 times: “Om Namah Shivaya” or the specific mukhi beej mantra.
  4. Wear close to the skin as a mala or bracelet (ideally touching the body).
  5. Avoid wearing during bath with harsh chemical soaps or while applying synthetic perfumes directly on beads.
Important: Higher mukhis (10–21) and powerful combinations should be worn only under expert guidance.

Do & Don’ts

Do

  • Wear gemstones so that the open back touches the skin.
  • Remove gemstones during surgery, radiation or intense medical procedures.
  • Clean and re-energize gemstones periodically (monthly or on full moon).
  • Use mantra, prayer and clear intention while wearing.

Don’t

  • Do not casually exchange gemstones that have been worn by others.
  • Do not mix too many strong astrology stones without guidance.
  • Do not use salt water on soft or porous stones (Pearl, Coral, Opal, Emerald).
  • Do not wear Rudraksha during intimate acts, funerals, or violent/negative environments.

Returns & Refunds Policy

Our 7-day inspection and return policy for damaged, defective, or mismatched natural gemstones and Rudraksha.

1. What we accept

We offer returns or replacements under the following verified conditions:

  • Wrong product sent (different physical item or variety than confirmed).
  • Product damaged in transit — cracked stones, broken jewelry mounts, missing components.
  • Shipment lost in transit by the carrier (confirmed by courier tracking audit).

2. What we do not accept

Because genuine gemstones and Rudraksha are natural geological and botanical items, the following are not eligible for return:

  • Minor natural inclusions, internal crystal veils, surface growth marks, or slight natural tone variations (hallmarks of Earth-mined authenticity).
  • Discolouration or physical damage resulting from misuse, abrasive chemicals, perfume exposure, salt-water immersion, or harsh cleaning.
  • Subjective change of mind after dispatch or delivery.
  • Custom-made jewelry settings, bespoke ring mountings, or energized stones.

3. Return & Refund Procedure

  1. Send an email to support@gemsofnature.shop or WhatsApp message to +91-7827491801 within 7 days of delivery with your order ID, photos of the package, and an unboxing video.
  2. If the issue qualifies, our team will provide return instructions and our verification facility address.
  3. Ship the item back in its original protective packaging, unused, unaltered, and with all lab certificates intact.
  4. Once received and inspected by our gemologists, refund processing completes within 5 business days.

4. Refund Method

Refunds are issued directly to the original payment method used during checkout (UPI, net banking, debit/credit card). For approved COD orders, refund is issued via bank NEFT/IMPS transfer upon account verification.

5. Shipping & Return Charges

Return shipping cost is borne by the customer, unless the return is due to our verified fulfillment error (wrong product or transit damage), in which case Gems of Nature provides prepaid return logistics.

6. Contact for Returns

Contact our support desk at support@gemsofnature.shop or WhatsApp +91-7827491801 with your order number. Our team will guide you through every step.

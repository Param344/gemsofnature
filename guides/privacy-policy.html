Data Protection & Trust

Privacy Policy

How Gems of Nature collects, protects, and respects your personal information.

Last updated: October 2026

At Gems of Nature, your trust is our highest priority. We are fully committed to protecting your personal data and being 100% transparent about how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information. This Privacy Policy explains your rights, our responsibilities, and the strict standards we follow to ensure your privacy is never compromised.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to operate our services safely and effectively, including:

  • Identity Information: name, phone number, email address.
  • Order Information: billing/shipping addresses, items purchased, order history.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device/browser type, pages visited, usage patterns (for security & analytics).
  • Payment Information: processed securely through third-party gateways; we do not store card details on our servers.

We never collect unnecessary or sensitive personal information unless required by law.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Processing and delivering orders;
  • Providing customer support and consultation follow-ups;
  • Sending essential updates about orders, deliveries or inquiry status;
  • Improving website functionality, performance and security;
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access;
  • Sending offers or recommendations only with your explicit consent.

We do not use your personal data for any purpose unrelated to your experience with Gems of Nature.

3. How We Protect Your Data

We apply industry-standard security measures to keep your information safe at all times, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption across the entire website;
  • Firewall & Cloudflare protection for threat detection and prevention;
  • Encrypted servers and restricted data access;
  • Regular security audits and system monitoring;
  • Zero storage of sensitive financial credentials on our servers.

4. Sharing of Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information — ever.

We only share information with trusted service partners who need it to perform essential fulfillment tasks, such as:

  • Shipping and courier logistics partners (DHL, FedEx, UPS, India Post EMS);
  • Payment processors (Razorpay, bank transfer gateways);
  • Analytics and security monitoring tools.

Each partner is contractually required to keep your information confidential and secure, and they are prohibited from using it for their own purposes.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (including applicable data privacy regulations), you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access – request a copy of your personal data;
  • Right to Correction – update or fix your information;
  • Right to Deletion – request removal of your data;
  • Right to Restrict Processing;
  • Right to Opt-Out of Marketing;
  • Right to Withdraw Consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@gemsofnature.shop.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to:

  • Enable secure site functionality and CSRF defense;
  • Maintain your shopping cart state;
  • Improve performance and asset caching;
  • Understand aggregate visitor engagement.

You can disable non-essential cookies via your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to complete your orders, provide ongoing support, and fulfill statutory tax and legal obligations.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. International Transfers

When fulfilling international orders, data is transmitted to international shipping networks solely to ensure smooth customs clearance and delivery.

10. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage regular review.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact:

Email: info@gemsofnature.shop

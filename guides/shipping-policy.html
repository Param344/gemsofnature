Delivery & Logistics

Shipping Policy

Flat ₹200 insured domestic courier delivery across India and transparent quote-first international dispatch.

1. Order Processing Time

Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–3 business days from our warehouse (unless otherwise mentioned on the specific product or consultation page). You will receive a dispatch confirmation email / WhatsApp message with full tracking details once your order has shipped.

2. Domestic Shipping (India)

  • We ship via reputed courier and insured parcel services across India.
  • Standard domestic shipping charge: ₹200 flat fee across India, ensuring insured, tamper-evident delivery.
  • Typical delivery timeframe: 4–10 business days depending on destination region, state, and courier performance.
  • Packing: We ensure secure packaging using protective bubble wrap, tamper-evident seals, and rigid boxes to protect natural gemstones and Rudraksha. Natural stones may possess minor internal inclusions or growth marks; these are authentic natural characteristics, not transit defects.

3. International Shipping (Quote-First Workflow)

  • We ship worldwide via reputable international courier services (DHL, FedEx, UPS, India Post EMS).
  • International shipping follows an owner quote-first workflow: customers submit an international shipping inquiry with their destination address, and our team provides an approved shipping quote before payment confirmation.
  • Estimated delivery time: 7–21 business days from dispatch (may vary due to destination customs clearance and postal carrier schedules).
  • Import duties, customs fees or local taxes (if applicable) are borne by the recipient in their destination country.
  • We declare accurate product values and HS codes but cannot control customs clearance timelines or local customs regulations once a package departs India.

4. Order Tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email and WhatsApp. Please use that tracking number to follow your order until delivery. If tracking does not update for more than 5 business days (domestic) or 10 business days (international), please reach out to us at support@gemsofnature.shop — our logistics team will liaise with the courier directly.

5. Lost or Damaged Shipments

  • If your package is delivered damaged, please retain all original packaging materials.
  • Record an unboxing video and take clear photographs of the packaging and damage within 48 hours of delivery, then contact our team.
  • Upon verification, we will arrange a replacement or full refund in accordance with our return guidelines.
  • For international orders, destination customs clearance delays are outside courier control; our team provides full documentation support to facilitate clearance.
